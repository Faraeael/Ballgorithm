performance or release authorization.

## 2. Accepted functionality

- Five canonical court slots with any eligible player permitted in any slot.
- `CourtLineup` as the public v5 lineup contract.
- Deterministic foul-out substitutions emitted by the simulator.
- Six-personal-foul disqualification.
- Mandatory eligible-bench replacement when a bench player exists.
- Depleted-roster continuation when no eligible inactive roster player exists.
- Legal substitution windows W0, W1, W2, W4, W5 and rejection of non-windows.
- Horn-edge pending-replacement handling, including made and missed final free throws and the phase-aware P1/P2 sequence.
- Deterministic replacement ranking: same-slot preference, first eligible roster-order fallback, and zero PRNG draws during selection.
- Player minutes derived from the validated event ledger.
- Exact five-player minutes conservation across regulation, overtime, W1 splits, continuation and five-player rosters.
- Full-roster box-score accounting and substitute statistical attribution.
- Player participation indicators on the mobile results screen.
- Starter/substitute ordering and explicit role labels.
- Disqualification and depleted-continuation disclosures.
- Historical compatibility boundaries: v5 rejects v4 ledgers explicitly; historical fixtures remain pinned to their own builds.
- Deterministic replay, canonical ledger pinning and worker/calling-isolate parity.

The simulator emits only foul-out substitutions. Routine tactical substitutions are ledger-valid but
are **not** generated by the simulator.
