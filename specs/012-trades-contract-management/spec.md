## 2. Trade assets and window

- Two different existing franchises, one the human franchise. Both packages nonempty. Support one or multiple contracted players, plus approved draft assets in T03. Players use `PlayerId` with expected active contract ID; picks use `(draftSeasonYear, overallPick)`; rights use `(draftSeasonYear, prospectId)` with the recorded selection slot checked. Typed asset kinds prevent namespace collisions. Also reject any duplicate underlying player/prospect, including across asset kinds and both packages.
- `openingOffseason` only, no regular-season state, no pending signing/market execution, clearance and hardship not started. Allow before draft starts, or after draft and required AI rookie decisions complete; never during draft execution. Player trades may occur before free agency starts or after it completes, not between rounds. No trade after clearance starts in this milestone: no resetting offers, reopening clearance or rebasing hardship authorizations.
- Only live rostered standard/two-way players with exactly one active employment and salary pair. Preserve category; reject free agents, retired players, training-camp players and unresolved or invalid ownership. Picks and rights have their own gates (§7). Recommended v1 anti-churn rule: an asset received by trade cannot be traded again in that offseason. Do not expose AI-to-AI, multi-party, or automated trade initiation.

## 3. Atomic transaction contract

1. Accept a canonical proposal with season, franchises, sorted assets, expected owners/contract links, and quote precondition. Reject repeated/empty/stale assets and wrong phase before building a candidate. Preview is read-only.
2. Build both teams' target rosters and all dependent finance/draft records from one immutable input. Remove outgoing before adding incoming. Keep unaffected roster order; append incoming in stable asset-ID order. Validate only the complete prospective state, never persist an intermediate state.
3. Validate model invariants and both financial/roster outcomes (§§4–5), then the deterministic AI decision (§6). Return a typed failure with side, asset and reason. Legal does not mean accepted. Test domain without a UI or storage dependency.
4. The controller serializes commit through its existing busy/recovery gates. Recompute using the live career; if the preview inputs or displayed outcomes changed, require fresh review. Append one deterministic receipt keyed by season and monotonic committed sequence. Include that receipt and all asset transfers in one save. Do not notify success or install the new in-memory career until the durable write succeeds.
5. A rejection writes nothing. A failed write keeps the prior visible state and locks mutation until reload. If storage completed an uncertain write, reload may return the **whole** new state; check receipt before retrying. Never blindly submit again. Same confirmation cannot publish a second receipt. Neither recovery outcome may show partial assets or payroll.

## 6. Deterministic AI acceptance

Separate legal validation, basketball value, term/cost, then strategy. Only the human can propose; the AI counterparty evaluates its own post-state. No random calls, external LLM, anti-scout reveal, or secret policy bypass.

- **Basketball:** compare a unique position-aware starting five and best five remaining standard rotation players before/after, weighting starters above reserves. Rank by visible overall, then canonical roster order and ID; fill missing positions with best remaining. Compute marginal value rather than summing the package. Do not credit more than two two-way prospects for upside; rank them using visible overall only. Penalize a lost natural-position option when depth is already thin. A top-two starter leaving triggers a separate quality gate: at least one incoming near-peer starter, or an approved high-pick package plus worthwhile cap relief for a rebuilding AI. Low-value bodies cannot substitute for either gate.
- **Finance:** calculate current and future actually signed cash/cap deltas, including missing-slot cost; use the same finance output as legal validation. A contract is not positive simply because it is long; reward productive years at an efficient salary, penalize expensive non-rotation years and future commitment risk. Require positive compensation before taking a net unfavorable guarantee package, not just legally affordable terms.
- **Strategy:** contenders value present starters and continuity more; rebuilding values visible age, controlled affordable years and draft assets more. Use one shared integer scale, with fixed strategy weights, slot weights, pick slot tiers and acceptance margins. Rights value uses approved **public** scouting bands/age/draft slot only; never private potential, hidden development traits, or scouting truth. A pick's value declines with later slot. Do not value future picks that do not exist.
- **Acceptance:** all hard gates pass, then net strategy-adjusted value exceeds a fixed positive margin; exact tie rejects. No automatic counteroffer. Stable reason order: legality → missing starter/position → guarantees → value. Show understandable reason codes and public deltas, not hidden evaluation inputs. Disallow same-offseason flip and require positive AI margin to discourage reversible profit; test repeated identical quotes and attempted reciprocal deals. Owner approved the exact integer weights, cutoff table and listed fixtures on 2026-11-26. These are design constraints, not calibrated claims.

## 7. Draft asset ownership

- **Unsigned rights:** transfer only the acquisition owner on the existing current-year or carried right. Use one authority based on origin; never duplicate in both ledgers or add a roster slot. Preserve year, slot, prospect and carried prospect snapshot. For current-year rights, require draft and AI signing decisions complete. Treat earlier AI decisions as historical evidence at their original owner; no rerun or transfer of a decision. A human recipient may use existing rookie signing when otherwise eligible. An AI recipient keeps the right unsigned and carries it forward; no new AI signing pass in this milestone. Signed or consumed rights are rejected.
- Separate original selecting franchise, signing franchise and current owner. Validate exact transfer lineage against the selection where present; an already-carried legacy entitlement with no historical selection uses the existing migrated ownership as its starting point. Use current acquisition owner for signing and carryover; obsolete owner cannot sign. Do not trust a new receipt to manufacture a missing right. Pick + player packages work pre-draft, as do carried rights + picks if all gates pass. No future-year pick inventory, swaps or protection.

## 8. Player contract management

- First slice: inspect current term, guarantee and expiry; communicate years remaining. On expiry let normal rollover release the player to the existing FA path, where the former franchise can submit a standard market offer under the same rules as others. No exclusive renewal, guaranteed re-sign, `extension` write, or contract restart. Do not append future-start pairs: active validation and rollover do not admit them. A true prospective extension would need an explicitly validated non-active commitment workflow and atomic activation at expiry; defer.
- **Waiver design; defer mutation.** Remove roster membership and active paid pair together, with an explicit release record. Carry each unpaid guaranteed salary, including the current year, to the releasing team as dead cash and applicable cap. Never set `retirementYear` to simulate release. To implement later, generalize liability origin/effective year under approved rules; decide current-year FA eligibility, prior acquisition joins and `PlayerContract.idFor(playerId, year)` collision if same-year re-signing is allowed. Preserve original team/term evidence and prevent double payroll. Fully guaranteed waiver creates a slot but does not create spendable cap/cash; waiving a standard player also creates another vacancy. Do not ship a cosmetic or cancel-salary release button.

## T03 approved numeric valuation policy

Owner approved this exact public-input policy on 2026-11-26. All values are integers; `~/` truncates toward zero. Rank players by visible overall descending, roster order, then ascending player ID. Assign the best unused natural starter to each slot in PG, SG, SF, PF, C order. Fill empty slots in the same order with the best unused standard players. Use the best five remaining standard players as reserves. Keep the pre-trade strategy fixed during comparison.

| Component | Approved calculation |
|---|---|
| Basketball `B` | For each state, let `q = max(overall − 50, 0)`. Sum `4q` per starter, `2q` per reserve, `q ~/ 4` for each of the top two two-way players, and `6` per natural position with at least two standard options. Empty slots add zero. Use post minus pre. |
| Control `C` | For each member of the modeled ten-player standard rotation, add `clamp(27 − age, 0, 6)` per signed future year paying at most `min(4,000, 100 × q)`. Exclude the current year. Use post minus pre. |
| Finance `F` | Sum pre minus post cash payroll from the current year through year + 4; add pre minus post minimum-roster reserve for the current year. Divide the result by 400. Report cap separately without adding savings twice. |
| Picks `P` | Materialized slots `1–5 / 6–14 / 15–30 / 31–45 / 46–60`: `96 / 64 / 36 / 14 / 5`. |
| Rights `R` | `P(original slot) ~/ 4 + clamp(23 − public age, 0, 5) + grade points`. Public grades `A+ / A / A- / B+ / B / B- / C+ / C`: `18 / 15 / 12 / 9 / 6 / 4 / 2 / 0`. Unknown legacy grade adds zero. |
| Draft `D` | Sum `P` and `R` for incoming assets, subtract outgoing asset values. Unsigned rights earn no rotation credit or roster slot. |
| Strategy | Latest archived regular-season rank: `1–8` contending, `9–20` middle, `21–30` rebuilding. Without archived standings, rank teams by sum of their modeled starters' overall ratings, descending; ascending team ID breaks a tie. |
| Score and margin | Contending: `(13B + 5C + 10F + 7D) ~/ 10`, margin `8`. Middle: `B + C + F + D`, margin `6`. Rebuilding: `(8B + 15C + 10F + 15D) ~/ 10`, margin `5`. Require strictly greater than margin; ties reject. |

Hard gates and public reason order:

1. Run trade legality first. Reject if a previously covered natural position loses its last standard option.
2. If either pre-trade top-two starter leaves, require a distinct incoming post-trade starter rated at least `departure overall − 5` for each departure. Pair strongest departure with strongest incoming starter first. Exception: rebuilding AI loses only one top-two starter, receives a materialized top-five pick, has `D ≥ 80`, and reduces current standard cap payroll by at least `800 CU`.
3. If guaranteed cash payroll plus current roster reserve increases across the five-year window, require the weighted non-financial score (same strategy formula with `F = 0`) to exceed `increase ~/ 400`. Use actual signed obligations. Cap, cash, staff, facilities, debt and hardship remain under the existing legality rules.
4. Apply the score and margin. Return the first failed gate. No counteroffers, RNG, hidden potential, development curves, or external evaluation.
